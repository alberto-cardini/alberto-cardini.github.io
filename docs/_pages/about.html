Research & experience

Mar – May 2026

Research Intern

Imaginalis · Florence

Adapted an IDDNet-based residual 3D U-Net in PyTorch for CBCT restoration. Developed data preparation, a composite training objective and Gaussian-weighted sliding-window inference for full volumes on an 8 GB GPU.

On the thesis evaluation set, the custom-loss model achieved +0.70 dB greater mean PSNR improvement and +0.016 greater mean SSIM improvement than RED-CNN. These correspond to relative gains of 14.83% and 10.46% in the improvements over noisy input.
